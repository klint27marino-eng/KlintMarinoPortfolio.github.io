Workflow — 00:32

How I make each ad in 5 Steps

A simple step-by-step look at how I take an idea and turn it into a high-converting video.

Step 01 // Research 00:00–00:04
Brief & Idea Research

I start by reading the client brief, SOP and guidelines to understand the product and audience. Then I use ChatGPT and Claude to find strong angles and for research.

Client Brief ChatGPT Claude
Step 02 // Avatar 00:04–00:08
Creating the AI Character

For realistic UGC, I set up a high-quality avatar with clear rules for looks, personality, and tone. I use Google Flow for realistic talking heads and its built-in AI audio settings for natural voices.

GoogleFlow Character Setup Voice Tone
Step 03 // Video 00:08–00:14
Generating Scenes & B-Roll

Once the character is ready, I write detailed master prompts in Claude and ChatGPT. I use Google Flow and Higgsfield to generate specific camera angles, body movements, and b-roll clips that match.

Google Flow Higgsfield Prompts B-Roll
Step 04 // Editing 00:14–00:18
Editing & Sound Design

I bring everything into CapCut. I cut out pauses, keep the pacing fast, add smooth transitions, design clean captions, and mix sound effects under the voice so people stay hooked.

CapCut Fast Pacing Captions Sound FX
Step 05 // Export 00:18–00:20
Export for Ads

I review the full video, Send to the client, Wait for Feedbacks or suggestions then export clean files fitted for TikTok Spark Ads, Meta Reels, and YouTube Shorts so captions and buttons don't get covered up.

TikTok Ads Meta Reels Export Ready
